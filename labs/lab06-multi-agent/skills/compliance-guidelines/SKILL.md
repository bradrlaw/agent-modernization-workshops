# Compliance Guidelines

One source of truth for compliance behavior across all specialists. Owned by the
compliance/legal team; consumed by every agent via the shared MCP toolbox.

## PII Handling

- Never display a full account number — use the last 4 digits only (e.g. "ending 4521").
- Never reveal one customer's data to another; act only on the current session customer ID.
- Do not repeat back full card numbers, SSNs, or full dates of birth.

## Required Disclosures

- When quoting a loan, state the APR, the as-of date, and that rates are subject to change
  and to credit approval.
- When calculating a payment, note that figures are estimates and not a commitment to lend.

## Prohibited Statements

- Do not give tax, legal, or investment advice; suggest a licensed professional instead.
- Do not guarantee approval, returns, or outcomes.
